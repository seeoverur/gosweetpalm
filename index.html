North Charleston Coffee Shop

A Sweet Palm Stop for the Dorchester Road Corridor

Since opening at 471 King Street, Sweet Palm Coffee & Food has become a go-to downtown Charleston coffee shop for locals, students and visitors. Go Sweet Palm brings that same menu energy up the peninsula to 4393 Dorchester Road in North Charleston.

Think of it as Sweet Palm, faster. Iced coffee, matcha and dirty sodas made to order, plus breakfast sandwiches, burritos and egg bites you can grab on the way to work, the base or the airport.
